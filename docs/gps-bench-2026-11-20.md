# Weak fixes indoors: a still board became carried

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Status:** measurements on the bench boards; the
floor it gives is a best guess (roadmap D234).

## What happened

Board `25f161d73112` lay still on a desk indoors through every bench
run. In the run that began 2026-11-18 its fixes strayed far enough, for
long enough, that it became carried (spec "Position"). A carried node
stores no place. Booted on 2026-11-20 with a build that gives none, and
with no fix at all that day, it had no place and sent no heartbeat, so
no relay knew it was there.

On 11-19 the fixes of `…3112` had a mean C/N0 of 11 to 25 dB-Hz, median
18, on 3 to 7 satellites, and the longest unbroken run beyond the
carried radius, 250 m, of their median was 58 fixes. The carried rule
needs 72 s.

## The floor

The L76K acquires a satellite down to −148 dBm, 18 dB below the −130 dBm
of an open sky, and tracks one it already has down to −162 dBm
(`docs/gps-schedule-2026-10-25.md`). An open sky reads about 40 dB-Hz
(`docs/gps-bench-2026-11-06.md`), so the acquisition limit is about
22 dB-Hz. A fix whose satellites average less is held on signals the
receiver could not have found, and the table shows what such fixes are
worth: the wander grows as the mean falls.

A fix is dropped when its mean is under 22 dB-Hz.

## What it does not settle

- On 11-17 the fixes of `…f4f5` at 22 dB-Hz and over lay up to 275 m
  off, so the floor does not bound the wander on every day.
- No board has been read under an open sky. If it reads well above
  40 dB-Hz the floor can rise.
- A node at a poor window now gets few fixes or none, and has no place
  until a map point is set in the app.
- `…3112` gave 55 587 fixes on 11-18, 7 600 on 11-19 and none in two
  hours on 11-20, in one place. The cause is not known.

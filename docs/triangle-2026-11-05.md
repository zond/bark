# The triangle rule: ending the search for an address no node is at

**Status:** research note, 2026-11-05. Measures the triangle rule
(roadmap D116), which was not adopted, 2026-11-06: with the addressed
node first in the order of spec "Next hop" (roadmap D116a,
`addressed-2026-11-06.md`) most of the searches it was for end at the
node, and on top of that the rule saves only about one percent of the
air of the texts written, for a geometric test at every routing (the
lost texts' share 2.7 → 1.4, 2.0 → 0.9 and 1.6 → 0.9 % at ×1, ×3 and
×4). Spec references are to `protocol.md` by section name.

## Summary

In the simulated city at the rate of spec "Capacity and sizing" and at
three and four times it, 0.55 to 1.0 % of the accepted texts end at the
hop cap, and those texts take 11 to 15 % of all the air: 41 to 51 s
each, against 2 to 4 s for a delivered one. Seven to nine in ten are
addressed to a carried node. The address is where the node was; it has
walked on, no relay owns the position, and the search walks the city to
the cap.

The triangle rule ends such a search where it circles the address: a
relay that, with two neighbours it has tried for the message, encloses
the destination position drops it. Measured on the same seeds against
main `e2e2761`, it cuts the air of lost texts by about a third (14.7 →
8.7 % of all air at the design rate, 14.0 → 9.7 % at three times, 11.4
→ 8.3 % at four), and a text it ends costs 7 to 12 s of air, not 41 to
51 s. It delivers fewer texts: 97.55 → 97.28 % of those accepted at the
design rate, 97.28 → 96.95 % at three times, 95.34 → 95.27 % at four,
where the air it saves shortens latency (p50 36.5 → 31.2 s). The texts
it loses are those to carried nodes, 1.5 to 1.8 points of them: on main
the walk to the cap finds the walker by chance about half the time, and
the rule ends that walk. The other scenarios are unchanged or within
their seed spread.

## The finding

Main `e2e2761`, `scenarios/dense-power.toml` seed 1, release build. ×1 is
6 300 s, its settled hour 2 700 to 6 300 s; ×3 and ×4 are 14 400 s, their
last hour 10 800 to 14 400 s, made as in `district-2026-11-04.md`, "Rate".
Texts are counted by when they were written, in that window.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ×1 | 18 234 | 0.55 % | 72, 24, 4 of 100 | 40.8 s | 2.00 s | 14.7 % |
| ×3 | 54 734 | 1.00 % | 500, 35, 12 of 547 | 45.2 s | 2.39 s | 14.0 % |
| ×4 | 72 319 | 0.92 % | 595, 58, 12 of 665 | 51.1 s | 3.99 s | 11.4 % |

A text at the cap took 255 hand-overs and 350 to 400 offers, against 10
to 12 for the median delivered text. Its destination is mostly a node that
was carried when the text was written: its address is the place it last
heartbeat as fixed, or a fix it has since walked away from. No relay
within 50 m of that place lacks a closer neighbour, or none is within
50 m at all, so nobody owns it (spec "Owner"), and the depth-first search
of spec "Why the search finds a path" visits the city until the cap ends
it.

The air share is over the whole run, from the first text; "air" is the
DATA frames that carried a text and the ACKs that answered them, which
the simulator counts per text (`sim.rs`, `message_frame`).

## The rule

As the branch built it (`ba527e4`):

- **When.** Each time a relay routes a message, before it chooses a
  candidate. A message is routed again here only after it comes back: a
  neighbour returned it, or answered seen. So the test falls when the
  second corner's return or seen arrives.
- **Corners.** The neighbours in the record's `handed` set, the parent
  left out (spec "Message records"): each has had the message and holds
  none, having returned it, answered seen, or offered a copy this relay
  answered seen. Only one that holds mail counts, neither sleepy nor
  carried: a moving node has no fixed place (spec "Position"). The relay
  is the third corner, and applies the rule only where it may own a
  destination near it: it holds mail, is past its first rotation and has
  a place (spec "Owner").
- **Test.** The triangle of the relay and any two corners holds the
  destination position, edges included; three on a line hold nothing.
  `geo::encloses` does it exactly in position counts: the local plane
  only scales each axis by a positive factor, which keeps the side of a
  line a point is on, and every cross product fits `i64` (offsets of at
  most 2²⁵ counts, products of at most 2⁵¹).
- **End.** The message is dropped, `DropReason::Triangle`, and nothing is
  sent for it: not forwarded, not returned to the parent. The simulator
  counts `triangle` among its drops and writes `Triangle` in the messages
  CSV.

The record needs nothing new: `handed` already holds the corners, and the
neighbour table their positions. `Relay` is unchanged in size.

## How it was measured

Release builds of main `e2e2761` and of the rule (`ba527e4`), the same
seeds, the same scenario files:

- the city, `dense-power.toml` seed 1: ×1 for 6 300 s, settled hour; ×3
  and ×4 for 14 400 s, last hour;
- the district, `district.toml` at ×32, seeds 1 to 3, 900 to 1 800 s;
- the design-rate set: `s4-capacity.toml` seeds 1 to 10 (settled hour),
  `ring.toml`, `corridor.toml` (390 to 1 800 s), `mesh-burst.toml` and
  `mesh-spread.toml` (390 to 2 700 s), seeds 1 to 10 each.

A text is accepted unless its origin refused it (pushback or every way
banned). Lost is every other drop. In flight is a text neither delivered
nor dropped when the run ends. Refusals told are refusing ACKs, from the
JSON summary.

**Matching texts across runs.** The two builds see the same traffic: the
same origins, destinations and times. A text is matched by its origin,
destination and the time it was written. A message's ID is not, since a
text due while its origin still holds the last is skipped, and the
skips differ between builds. From the first frame that differs the two
runs part, so a single text's fate differs between them for reasons of
timing alone; both directions are therefore shown.

## The island cost

The texts the rule ended, matched on main:

| run | build | texts the rule ended, their fate on main | texts main lost that the rule delivered |
|---|---|---|---|
| city ×1 | rule | delivered 73, HopCap 55, in flight 7 | HopCap 34, ReturnFailed 2, NoRoute 1, Unplugged 1 |
| city ×3 | rule | delivered 326, HopCap 221, in flight 8 | HopCap 249, Unplugged 3, Banned 2, ReturnFailed 2, Budget 2 |
| city ×4 | rule | delivered 471, HopCap 247, in flight 26, ReturnFailed 2, not matched 2, Unplugged 1, Pushback 1 | HopCap 304, Unplugged 13, ReturnFailed 10, Banned 7, NoRoute 5, Budget 1 |
| district ×32 | rule | delivered 87, in flight 79, HopCap 26, not matched 13, Pushback 1 | HopCap 19, Banned 16, ReturnFailed 4, NoRoute 1, QueueExpired 1 |
| S4 | rule | HopCap 9, delivered 7, in flight 2, ReturnFailed 1 | HopCap 47, NoRoute 13, Banned 12, ReturnFailed 8 |

On main, about half of these texts arrive: a median of 36 hand-overs
for them at ×3 (31 at ×4) against 10 (12) for all delivered texts, a
tenth after 248 (237) or more. The walk that would have run to the cap
meets the carried node itself, which owns any message for its ID wherever it is (spec
"Owner"), or a relay now within 50 m of the address. The rule ends that
walk. The reverse happens too, by the runs parting: at ×3, 249 texts main
lost at the cap arrive with the rule, at ×4 304. The net is what the
delivered share shows.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| city ×1 | main | 93.54 % | 96.97 % | 99.55 % |
| city ×1 | rule | 91.76 % | 96.85 % | 99.58 % |
| city ×3 | main | 92.10 % | 97.77 % | 99.60 % |

So the cost falls on texts to carried nodes, by 1.5 to 1.8 points; the
others are unchanged at ×1 and ×3 and gain 0.2 to 0.4 points at ×4,
from the air saved. The "island" of the decision, a node behind
relays inside the triangle that none of the corners reaches, is not
what the runs show; what they show is a walk that finds a moving node by
chance.

A tenth of the texts the rule ended at ×3 took 9 hand-overs or fewer on
main: the corners met in this run where in the other the owner was
reached first, or a corner had answered seen with its own search still
open. Spec "Next hop" names both among the rule's costs.

## A variant, measured and not built

Since a corner that answered seen may still have a neighbour untried
that leads to the node, one variant was run beside the rule: the
triangle ends the message only if no live neighbour this relay has not
tried could own the destination, none with its ID and none holding mail
within 50 m of it. Same runs:

Ring, corridor, mesh burst and spread mesh are unchanged with the
variant too. It ends fewer texts (0.59 to 0.75 % of accepted against
0.74 to 1.04 %), loses a half to two thirds as many deliveries to
carried nodes, and saves about three quarters of the air the rule saves. It costs a
pass over the neighbour table at each test. It is not in the core.

## What it shows

- The texts lost at the cap are mostly to carried nodes, and each costs
  about twenty delivered texts' air.
- The rule ends them within tens of hand-overs (43 to 69 on average in
  the city), at 7 to 12 s of air each, and cuts the air of lost texts by about a third.
- It delivers fewer texts in the city, 0.27 and 0.33 points at ×1 and ×3
  and 0.07 at ×4; the loss is on texts to carried nodes, and at ×4 the
  air saved wins part of it back on the others.
- At ×1 the city's delivered share falls by 0.27 points, inside the seed
  spread of about ±0.3 points (roadmap D96, D113), but the fall is on one
  kind of destination at every city rate, and in each district seed the
  share of texts to carried nodes that end lost rises (0.65 → 3.94,
  2.28 → 6.02, 1.15 → 2.90 %), so it is not noise.
- Latency and refusals are unchanged within a few per cent up to ×3; at
  ×4 latency falls (p50 36.5 → 31.2 s, p90 193 → 171 s), refusals told
  fall a tenth, and refusals at the origin rise 0.58 → 0.71 %.

## What it does not show

- One seed per city rate: the city's spread at ×3 and ×4 is not known.
- Whether the air saved turns into delivered texts at higher load: at
  ×32 in the district delivery rises 82.12 → 82.88 %, within the seeds'
  spread of 79.5 to 86.6.
- A walker's own fate is the simulator's walk model; how often a real
  walk crosses the search is not measured.
- What finds a carried node instead. The rule makes a text to a node
  that has walked away fail fast rather than fail slowly or, half the
  time, arrive slowly; nothing in this note tells the origin.

## Verdict

Not adopted (status line). Alone, the rule as built spends about a third less air on
texts that end lost and loses 1.5 to 1.8 points more of the texts to
carried nodes, which on main arrive about half the time after a long
walk. The variant keeps three quarters of the saving at about half the
loss.

`addressed-2026-11-06.md` measures the rule with the addressed node first
in the order of spec "Next hop": the cost to carried nodes is gone there.

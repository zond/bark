# The busy estimate's rate, measured

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Status:** measured; one channel a second adopted
as roadmap D86. The bench note `bench-2026-10-30.md` has the radio's
costs this rests on.

## The question

A correct reading of another channel is 2.5 ms away from the home
channel: a retune (0.67 ms), four symbol periods (1.02 ms), the read
(0.16 ms) and a retune back. Spec "Busy estimate" swept every channel
each second on the assumption that a reading took 0.5 ms, short enough
to lose no packet. At 2.5 ms each reading loses the packets whose
preamble starts in a 1.5 ms window around it.

| | (a) every channel each second | (b) one channel a second |
|---|---|---|
| readings a second | 13 | 1 |
| deaf | 3.3 % | 0.25 % |
| packet starts lost to the sweep | 1.9 % | 0.15 % |
| window | 10 minutes, α = 1/600 | 30 minutes, α = 1/138 |
| samples a channel in a window | 600 | 138 |
| readings a channel in the first rotation, 195 s | 195 | 15 |

## How it was measured

The simulator's radio costs were set to the bench's first (commit "Sim:
the radio's costs as the bench measured them"): a retune 668 µs, a
reading of another channel 668 + 1024 + 155 + 668 µs, a CAD from
listening to listening 0.75 + 4.64 + 1.30 ms at the 2 ms oscillator
start (D87), a transmit 2.05 ms before its first symbol and 1.30 ms
back to listening. Main (`df65e3c`) charged 86 µs, 472 µs, 2.35 ms and
0.18 ms for the same.

Each option ran at these costs and at those of the SPI bus driven
blocking, an experiment the bench note reports and no decision has
taken yet: a retune 461 µs, the read 49 µs, a reading 2.0 ms, back to
listening 0.84 ms, CAD preparation 0.49 ms, a transmit 1.32 ms before
its first symbol; the CAD itself unmeasured on that bus and kept.

Scenarios: `s4-capacity.toml` at the design rate (×1) and at three times
it (×3, `per_leaf_per_hour` 2.875), `dense-power.toml` seed 1. Figures
are texts delivered of those sent in the settled hour.

## Results

| | main | (a) | (b) | (a) blocking bus | (b) blocking bus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S4 ×1, seed 1 | 93.4 % | 93.4 % | 93.6 % | 93.4 % | 93.6 % |
| S4 ×3, seed 1 | 62.0 % | 38.7 % | 47.9 % | 42.5 % | 45.0 % |
| S4 ×3, seed 2 | | 36.5 % | 45.2 % | | |
| S4 ×3, seed 3 | | 39.3 % | 43.6 % | | |
| `dense-power.toml`, seed 1 | 97.9 % | 78.9 % | 80.9 % | 78.6 % | 84.5 % |

At the design rate the options deliver alike, as main does: the network
is far from its limits and a lost packet is sent again. Over the run
the relays lost 125 000 receptions to retunes and readings under (a)
and 23 000 under (b).

At three times the rate (b) delivers 7.4 points more on average over
three seeds, and 2.5 points more on the blocking bus. The difference is
what the sweep's deafness costs a network at its limit, where every
lost packet is a retry that takes channel time from others.

Most of the fall from main at ×3 is not the sweep's. With the sweep's
costs alone and the CAD and transmit costs of main, seed 1 delivers
51.7 % under (a) and 55.7 % under (b); with every cost, 38.7 and
47.9 %. A listen-before-talk is a CAD and a reading of the channel, two
excursions with a return to listening between, 9.2 ms, where main
charged 2.8 ms; each hand-over and each answer pays it. Making that
cheaper is a later item: the blocking bus takes a third off the
commands, and the reading and the CAD need not return to listening
between them.

The ring (`ring.toml`, sleepy nodes listening, seeds 1 to 40) delivers
0.964 to 0.986 of its settled phase under (a), mean 0.976, and 0.958 to
0.982 under (b), mean 0.971, the same number of texts on average (3200
and 3194): under (b) the relays at their duty cap take more from their
origins and give more back. The test's bound goes from 0.96 to 0.95.

## What (b) does to the choice at boot

A node without a stored home chooses at the end of its first rotation
(spec "Channel at boot") on 15 readings a channel, ±8 points on a 10 %
channel. That keeps it off a channel an interferer or a crowd of relays
holds and does not rank quiet channels; the move rule ranks them on its
30-minute window. In S4 ×1 the relays changed their home 28 times under
(b), boot choices and moves together, against 55 under (a); the move
rule moved them 9 times against 2.

`dense-power.toml` seed 1 does not storm on main: 97.9 % delivered and
295 000 s of airtime, the normal figure (roadmap D77 measured a storm at
90.95 % and 1 006 409 s against 272 306 s). At the measured costs it
storms under either rate: 78.9 % under (a) and 80.9 % under (b), its
airtime 2 030 000 s in both. Why the measured costs set it off is a
later item.

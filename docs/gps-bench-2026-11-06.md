# GPS on the bench, 2026-11-06

Three boards, `bae6e6cbf4f5`, `25f161d73112` and `7572482fc367`, on one
desk indoors near a window, metres apart, all on USB and so all relays.
Their serial logs run from the evening of 2026-11-05 to midday
2026-11-06, with reflashes at about 10:43, 11:12, 11:24, 12:12 and
12:21. Every true fix on all three lies within about 100 m of
59.2149° N, 18.2128° E.

## Good fixes dropped

**Seen.** All three boards logged `gps: fix … beyond every known relay;
dropped` for fixes at the desk, `bae6e6cbf4f5` 101 s after its 12:21
boot, `25f161d73112` 13 s, 37 s and 74 s after boots and at 6424 s and 8372 s
of one run, `7572482fc367` at 97 s and 117 s. After their reboots of the
day each reported its address as `0.000000 0.000000` with one or two relays
known, while before them, knowing no relay, they had taken fixes at the
desk. Their stored state read:

| board | stored place | carried |
|---|---|---|
| `bae6e6cbf4f5` | 50.4501° N, 30.5234° E, given at an earlier build, 1250 km away | yes |
| `25f161d73112` | 0, 0 | yes |
| `7572482fc367` | 59.2148° N, 18.2131° E, its first fix at the desk | yes, after far fixes indoors |

**Cause.** A node had a place when it had any stored state (roadmap
D83). But a carried node does not boot at its stored place, which is
where it stood when it was last fixed; it booted carried at the position
it was built with, 0, 0 on a board built without one. From there:

- it rebuilt every neighbour's short position near 0, 0, so each true
  fix at the desk lay thousands of kilometres from every relay it knew
  and was dropped;
- it heartbeated 0, 0, whose short form its neighbours rebuilt as a
  point up to half a period, 19.5 km, from themselves, so they dropped
  their own fixes too once those were the relays they knew;
- carried, its stored place never changed, so every reboot did it again.

A board accepted a fix only while it knew no relay, which is how
`bae6e6cbf4f5` and `7572482fc367` had addresses at the desk before.

**Fix.** A node has a place stored only as a fixed node (roadmap D133).
One stored as carried and built without a place boots with none: it
sends no heartbeat, checks its first fix against no neighbour, and takes
that fix as its place, fixed, rebuilding the neighbours it heard near it
(`bark-proto/tests/first_fix.rs`, `a_node_stored_as_carried_has_no_place`).
The three boards heal at their next boot on this firmware.
